\begin{enumerate}
    \item[\textbf{5.5.7.}] Studies have shown that the risk of developing coronary disease increases with the level of obesity, or accumulation of body fat. A study was conducted on the effect of exercise on losing weight. Fifty men who exercised lost an average of 11.4 lb, with a standard deviation of 4.5 lb. Construct a 95\% CI for the mean weight loss through exercise. Interpret the result and state any assumptions you have made.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.8.}] Raising findings on 60 successful pregnancies involving natural birth, an experimenter found that the mean pregnancy term was 274 days, with a standard deviation of 14 days. Construct a 90\% CI for the true mean pregnancy term $\mu$.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.9.}] Let $Y$ be a Binomial random variable with parameters $p$ and $n=400$. If the observed value of $Y$ is $y=120$, find a 95\% CI for $p$.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.10.}] For a health screening in a large company, the diastolic and systolic blood pressures of all the employees were recorded. In a random sample of 150 employees, 12 were found to suffer from hypertension. Find 95\% and 98\% CIs for the proportion of the employees of this company with hypertension.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.11.}] In a random sample of 500 items from a large lot of manufactured items, there were 40 defectives.
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item[\textbf{(a)}] Find a 90\% CI for the true proportion of defectives in the lot.
        \item[\textbf{(b)}] Is the assumption of normal approximation valid?
        \item[\textbf{(c)}] Suppose we suspect that another lot has the same proportion of defectives as in the first lot. What should be the sample size if we want to estimate the true proportion within 0.01 with 90\% confidence?
    \end{enumerate}
    \item[\textbf{5.5.12.}] Pesticide concentrations in sediment from irrigation areas can provide information required to assess the exposure and fate of these chemicals in freshwater ecosystems and their likely impacts on the marine environment. In a study (M\"{u}ller, J.F., et al., 2000. Pesticides in sediments from Queensland irrigation channels and drains. Mar. Pollut. Bull.\ 41 (7--12), 294--301), 103 sediment samples were collected from irrigation channels and drains in 11 agricultural areas of Queensland. In 74 of these samples, they detected DDT with concentration levels up to 840 ng/g dw. Obtain a 95\% CI for the proportion of total number of sediments with detectable DDT.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.13.}] Let $\overline{X}$ be the mean of a random sample of size $n$ from an $N(\mu,16)$ distribution. Find $n$ such that $P(\overline{X}-2<\mu<\overline{X}+2)=0.95$.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.14.}] Let $X$ be a Poisson random variable with parameter $\lambda$. A sample of 180 observations from this population has a mean equal to 2.5. Construct a 98\% CI for $\lambda$.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.15.}] An opinion poll conducted in March of 1996 by a newspaper (\textit{Tampa Tribune}) among eligible voters with a sample size 425 showed that the president, who was seeking reelection, had 45\% support. Give a 95\% and a 98\% CI for the proportion of support for the president.
    \item[\textbf{5.5.16.}] A random sample of 100 households located in a large city recorded the number of people living in each household, $Y$, and the monthly expenditure for food, $X$. The following summary statistics are given:
    \[
        \sum_{i=1}^{100} Y_i = 340
    \]
    \[
        \sum_{i=1}^{100} Y_i^2 = 1650
    \]
    \[
        \sum_{i=1}^{100} X_i = 40{,}000
    \]
    \[
        \sum_{i=1}^{100} X_i^2 = 44{,}000{,}000
    \]
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item[\textbf{(a)}] Form a 95\% CI for the mean number of people living in a household in this city.
        \item[\textbf{(b)}] Form a 95\% CI for the mean monthly food expenses.
        \item[\textbf{(c)}] For each case just given, interpret the results and state any assumptions you have made.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item[\textbf{5.5.17.}] Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be a random sample from an exponential distribution with parameter $\theta$. A sample of 350 observations from this population has a mean equal to 3.75. Construct a 90\% CI for $\theta$.
\end{enumerate}
