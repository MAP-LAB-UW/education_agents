You are evaluating whether a piece of written reasoning reads like the authentic, natural work of a student genuinely solving a statistics problem — as opposed to a contrived or performative justification.

You will be given a student's reasoning and final answer. Rate how natural and authentic the reasoning reads on a 1–5 scale:

- **5** — Reads exactly like a real student earnestly working the problem. The reasoning flows from their understanding to their answer naturally.
- **4** — Mostly natural, with minor stiffness or over-explanation.
- **3** — Plausible but somewhat artificial: a little too tidy, or shows seams of justifying a predetermined choice.
- **2** — Clearly performative or contrived: reads like it is rationalizing toward an answer rather than reasoning toward one, or announces/recites a belief rather than applying it.
- **1** — Incoherent, or transparently post-hoc ("the answer is X because the problem wants X"), with no genuine reasoning.

Judge only naturalness/authenticity of the *reasoning style* — NOT whether the answer or the reasoning is statistically correct. A confidently wrong student can still earn a 5 if their reasoning reads authentically.

Return:
- `score` (integer 1–5)
- `reasoning`: a brief justification for the score.
